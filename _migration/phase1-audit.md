## Splits I applied to HIGH-anchor clusters where `variant_products` linked unrelated silhouettes

Same pattern you approved for Iron Shell Skullie. The `variant_products` metafield in Shopify sometimes lists products that share a "look" but are structurally different products.

- **Chained Baby Tee Midnight cluster (8)** → split into `Chained Baby Tee` (4 colors) + `Cropped Chain Tank` (4 colors). Different silhouettes.
- **Signature Wideleg Sweats cluster (7)** → split into `Signature Wideleg Sweats` (3), `Form Straight Leggings` (2), `Morph Wide Leg Sweats` (2, merged with the loner Morph Grey), and `Vintage Sweats Black with White Stripes` → loner. Four different silhouettes were stapled together.
- **Chrome Thermal cluster (6)** → split into `Chrome Thermal` (4) + `Hombre Thermal` (2). Different product lines.
- **Iron Therapy Long-Sleeve Compression cluster (3)** → `Signature Long Sleeve Compression` (3, added Black w/ White Text), Iron Therapy → loner. Iron Therapy is its own drop.
- **Iron Therapy Short-Sleeve Compression cluster (3)** → same as above for short sleeve.
- **Tee cluster (4)** → `Stitched Cut Off Tee` (3), Faded Tee → loner. Faded Tee is not a cut-off.
- **Crewneck cluster (2)** → both to loners per your instruction (Forest Green Crewneck, Cherry Blossom Crewneck).

## Naming clean-ups I applied

- Generic auto-generated names like "Pack", "Print", "Track Jacket" were replaced with the specific style name (e.g. `Beater 3-Pack`, `Waffleknit Pullover`, `Prime Layer Track Jacket`).
- The `Sizes` (plural) option name on 4 products (Cropped Chain Tank Creme, three Hyper Mesh Shorts) will be normalized to `Size` during the rebuild.
- Beater 3-Packs consolidated into ONE family of 6 (Dusk, Vintage Washed, Nightshade, Neutral, Flame, Graffiti).
- Lightweight Tee 3-Packs consolidated into ONE family of 6 (Weighted, Supercharged, Nightshade, Neutral, Smoke, Butterfly).
